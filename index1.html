Before you hand over any cash

Buying from a classmate is friendly, but a book is still a thing you should look at first. Five quick habits keep both sides happy.

  • Meet in a public spot on campus.
  • Flip every page you care about.
  • Check it is the edition your teacher listed.
  • Pay only after you have checked.
  • Bring a friend if you like. Nobody minds.
